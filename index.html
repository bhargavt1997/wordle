📖 How to Play

Objective

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries or less.

Tile Colors

W Correct - Right letter, right spot.
O Present - Right letter, wrong spot.
R Absent - Letter not in word.

🏆 Scoring System

  • Fewer Guesses = More Points: Base points range from 1,000 (1st guess) to 100 (6th guess).
  • Speed Bonus ⏱️: Earn 50 points for every 10 full seconds remaining when you solve it!
  • Discovery Bonus 🔍: Finding Green and Yellow letters early gives you extra points instantly, even if you don't solve the word yet!
